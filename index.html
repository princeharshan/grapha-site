Write → Crystalize → Do → Review

You leave a rough plan. You come back to a better one.

A label is the whole setup. Put one on a document and it gets tended: between your visits the agent reads what you wrote and the rest of that label’s notes, then leaves its work in the margin. Your prose is never touched until you say yes.

The boundary

It reads the documents you label. Nothing else.

A label is permission. Put one on a document and your agents may read it; leave it off and they cannot. That is not a polite instruction in a prompt — it is enforced where the files are handed over, the same place the database connections are made read-only.

  • Unlabelled is invisible. Not read, not summarised, not indexed, not sent.
  • You can see the scope of any run. Which documents were in it, and what came back.
  • Nothing goes through us. Your agent runs on your Mac, on your own subscription. There is no Grapha server to send it to.

Live data

The number in the sentence comes from your database.

Connect Postgres, MySQL, SQLite, Mongo or Redis and a figure in your prose is a real query. Your Mac runs it, read-only, with the password in the Keychain. The agent writes the SQL and never sees a credential.

  • The last value stays written in the file, so the note reads with no app and no connection.

Frequently asked

Do I need a Claude or ChatGPT plan too?

Yes. Grapha drives the agent you already have, on your own subscription or API key. We do not resell tokens, which is what lets the app cost one payment instead of a monthly fee.

What happens on day 31?

The agents go quiet. Everything else keeps working: every note, label, chart, diagram, database and the whole editor. Nothing you wrote goes anywhere.

How is this different from Obsidian with a plugin?

Two things. Review: an agent’s work arrives as hunks you accept or reject against a snapshot of the file, so it works whichever tool did the editing. And live data: a figure in your prose can be a real query against your own database, cached into the file so it still reads without either.

Can an agent read notes I didn’t mean to share?

No. It is handed the documents carrying the label you gave it and nothing else. Everything unlabelled is invisible to it, and you can see which documents were in scope for any run.

What happens if you disappear?

Nothing changes. Your notes are markdown in ~/Grapha, and the licence is checked once at unlock and never again, so nobody can switch the app off remotely.

Windows or iPhone?

Mac only. Your notes are ordinary markdown files, so any app on your phone that reads markdown will read them today.
